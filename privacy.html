Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers "Axiomotion Pipeline", a private tool used by the Axiomotion channel owner to manage the Axiomotion YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.

What data the tool accesses

Through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, and only for the Axiomotion channel, the tool accesses:

  • channel settings and branding, to keep the channel profile up to date;
  • the channel's own videos and their metadata, to upload and schedule videos;
  • the channel's analytics and revenue reports, to measure how videos perform;
  • public comments on the channel's videos, read only, to collect viewer questions and corrections.

The tool also reads public statistics of public YouTube videos (titles, view counts, upload dates) to study topic trends.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to produce and improve Axiomotion videos. It is stored on the channel owner's own computer. Authorization tokens are kept locally and are never published or shared.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Locally stored data is deleted on request.
